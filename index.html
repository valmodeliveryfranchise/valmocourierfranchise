Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Valmo Delivery Franchise

What is Valmo Delivery Franchise?
Valmo Delivery Franchise is a logistics partnership program with Meesho, India's largest social commerce platform. Franchise partners operate delivery hubs and earn ₹70,000 to ₹80,000 per month.
What is the investment required for Valmo franchise?
The investment required to start a Valmo Delivery Franchise ranges from ₹1 to ₹2 Lakhs, making it one of the most affordable logistics franchise opportunities in India.
How much can I earn with Valmo franchise?
Valmo franchise partners can earn between ₹70,000 to ₹80,000 per month depending on the volume of orders processed and operational efficiency.
What support does Valmo provide to franchise partners?
Valmo provides comprehensive support including training, technology infrastructure, operational guidance, and ongoing assistance to help franchise partners succeed.
How do I apply for Valmo Delivery Franchise?
You can apply by filling the franchise application form on this page. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to guide you through the next steps.
